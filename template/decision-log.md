# Decision log

**Only if INTAKE Q2 = judgment** — feedback that is delayed, noisy, and probabilistic (trading, diagnosis, investing, management). Delete this file otherwise.

Outcomes are a corrupted signal here: a correct decision can lose, a reckless one can win, and volume alone teaches superstition. This file builds a clean signal by grading **process separately from outcome**. It is the most important artifact in a judgment domain.

**Before you act — write it and lock it:** the thesis in one sentence; what would prove you **wrong**; confidence as a number; what you're risking and why that size.

**After the outcome window closes — this order is the whole point:**

1. Grade the **process** with the outcome hidden. Did you follow your own rules? Was the thesis reasonable on what you knew then?
2. *Then* look at the outcome.
3. Quadrant: `good/good` repeat · `good process / bad outcome` variance, change nothing · `bad process / good outcome` **the dangerous one — you got paid for a mistake** · `bad/bad` fix.

**Monthly:** bucket by confidence and check calibration. If the 80%s happen 55% of the time, you are overconfident by a measurable amount. Take the `bad process` rows to `error-log.md` — the *why* belongs there.
